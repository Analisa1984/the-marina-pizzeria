Our Heritage

We are a family-owned Pizzeria that started in 1870 on the sun-drenched coasts of Sorrento, Italy. After 30 beautiful years in Italy, we fell in love with Whitby and decided to bring our heritage across the sea.

Today, we carry the same handed-down recipes and fresh ingredients into every pizza, stretched with our heritage and love. We enjoy making our traditional pizzas very much and we love to serve you. Come dine with us and taste a piece of Sorrento right here in Whitby.
